Project overview

Individual Tree Crown Segmentation

This project contributes to an end-to-end individual tree crown instance segmentation pipeline supervised by Prof. Yan Guangjian at Beijing Normal University. It does not currently have a DOI or manuscript download, so this page provides a concise project overview based on the CV material and local segmentation outputs.

Research task

Dense crown delineation from high-resolution imagery

The project focuses on segmenting individual tree crowns in dense forest and complex urban forest scenes, where adjacent crowns, shadow, crown overlap, and variable canopy texture make boundary extraction difficult.

Data basis

ISPRS benchmark conversion and local datasets

My role includes processing benchmark data from the International Society for Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing, converting annotation formats for model training, and preparing train/validation imagery for repeatable experiments.

Model route

YOLOv8-seg and YOLOv11 segmentation workflow

I contributed to implementation around YOLOv8-seg and YOLOv11 architectures, including data augmentation, hyperparameter tuning, experiment comparison, and iterative inspection of dense-crown predictions.

Post-processing

Cleaner masks for complex forest scenes

The workflow includes post-processing steps such as noise filtering and crown boundary smoothing to refine segmentation outputs and improve usability in visually complex canopy conditions.

Pipeline

How the project turns imagery into usable crown masks

  1. 01 Prepare benchmark imagery

    Convert ISPRS-style labels and imagery into training-ready segmentation formats.

  2. 02 Train segmentation models

    Run YOLOv8-seg and YOLOv11 experiments with controlled augmentation and tuning.

  3. 03 Compare dense-crown outputs

    Inspect prediction quality against crown masks in crowded and heterogeneous scenes.

  4. 04 Refine boundaries

    Apply filtering and boundary smoothing so segmentation outputs are easier to use downstream.
